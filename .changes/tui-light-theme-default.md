- The terminal's background now decides the default theme: a light terminal gets the light palette (the editor surface and your echoed messages are light instead of dark bands on a light page), a dark one keeps Prime. `/settings` shows the theme actually in use, and the Theme submenu opens on it. Text on the theme's own surfaces (the prompt, your echoed messages, custom-message cards) no longer inherits the terminal's foreground colour, so it stays readable whatever palette the terminal itself uses.
