- The prompt and your echoed turns follow the terminal's own colour scheme: they paint no background and use the terminal's foreground, so they stay readable on light and dark terminals alike (previously a fixed surface colour made the input area and the echo invisible whenever the terminal disagreed with it, down to 1.03:1 contrast). The `> ` lead marks your turn. The bundled `prime`/`dark`/`light` palettes still drive the rest of the interface and `/settings` shows the one in use.
